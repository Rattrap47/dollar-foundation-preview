Get involved

You don’t need a reason to be kind. Here’s how you can help.

Give

Donate

Your gift buys provisions for outreaches, supports needy students with what school demands, and helps us reach more orphans, older people and widows.

To give, just get in touch. Call or WhatsApp us on +233 24 603 0338 (Ghana) or +1 (743) 767-0950 (USA), email richardamoakotvl@gmail.com, or message us on Facebook, and we’ll tell you how to give.

Gifts in kind

These are the kinds of things we take to schools and communities, and we welcome donations of any of them:

  • Books, pens and pencils
  • Water and drinks
  • Clothing, shoes and slippers
  • Bags

…and many more.

Please get in touch before dropping anything off, so we can agree the best way to get it to us.

Give your time

Our outreaches are run by volunteers. Whatever your age or skills, there’s a place for you.

Volunteer at outreaches

Help plan, pack and share provisions, and spend time with the people we visit.

Mentor a student

Share your experience with a student at primary, JHS, SHS or tertiary level. Professionals, graduates and university students are all welcome.

Partner with us

Schools, churches, mosques, businesses and community groups — let’s plan an outreach together.
